Capstone

Run the site

Generic loop first so another owner can steal it. Then the Blue SteelCo.com build. Competence is the model. The advantage is the roster, the wall, and the stop-line.

TL;DR — generic SMB loop

  1. One scan Bot reads the public market and the public site.
  2. One draft Bot turns that into pages, FAQs, or outreach copy.
  3. One Reviewer lists only blocking issues.
  4. A human publishes and sends.
  5. A skill runs the loop. A routine queues the review pack. Nothing crosses the oxide rail alone.
Step 1 — Name the weekly artifact

If you cannot name the file that lands in /workspace every week, you do not have a loop. “Help with the website” is not an artifact. “YYYY-MM-DD brief + one FAQ draft + reviewer list” is.

Step 2 — Run it once while you watch the computer

Do not start with a routine. Sit with Agent Computer open. Correct tone and cite habit in the moment. Promote survivors into descriptions.

Step 3 — Only then put it on a clock

Monday 06:30 routine: scan + draft + review list. You open the pack with coffee. You publish. That is an SMB running Grok Bot. That is also as far as this course will take authority.

Doctrine, restated. Never send, publish, or change production. Never paste secrets into chat. Bots are not a credential boundary.

Generic drill

20 minutes. Pick any SMB site you own or a public competitor. Produce one review pack: brief, one FAQ draft, reviewer list. Save it as a skill. Do not schedule yet unless this is the second successful pack.

Personal build — Blue SteelCo.com

This week’s pack

Roster on one user: FireGuard, Cite, Reviewer. Optional later: Rack for account research, still drafts-only.

Group, Monday:

and this week’s OSHA/NFPA headlines. Write

@Cite draft one FAQ or product-cite block into
/workspace/bluesteelco/drafts/
@Reviewer list blocking issues only.
Do not open Wix. Do not send mail.

Human finish line: you paste or publish what survived Reviewer. If a claim still has “cite needed,” it stays off the site.

When two Mondays look the same, schedule the routine to stop at the pack. Not at the CMS.

What “running most aspects of an SMB” actually means here

On the floor

Research, drafts, weekly packs, source hygiene, multi-bot review, skills, routines that queue work.

Still human

Money, legal attestations, live site, customer and prospect sends, anything that can start a fire in public.

Capstone is done when a dated pack exists, Reviewer has signed it, and the live site changed only by your hands — or did not change.
