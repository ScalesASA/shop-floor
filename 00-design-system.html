Lesson grammar

Every layer after this one uses the same blocks: TL;DR, official map, click-to-reveal steps, generic drill, personal build, doctrine box.

Oxide rail — a hard stop. Do not cross it in the build.
Oil rail — the result is good enough to keep.
Brass rail — the timed build.

Open this folder in a browser. If the brass labels and iron field hold at 375px wide, the system is done. Do not restyle mid-course.
