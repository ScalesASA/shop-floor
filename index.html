A course in standing AI teammates

Grok Bot for an SMB floor

Eight layers. Each one is a twenty-minute explainer plus a build. The teaching is generic so any owner can run it. The worked shop is Blue SteelCo — industrial safety equipment, a live site, and a rule that nothing ships without a human.

Doctrine. Never send, publish, or change production. Never paste secrets into chat. Bots share one computer — they are not a credential boundary.
