Explainer

Plugins and computer use

Plugins are the structured path into Gmail, Notion, Slack, and the rest. Computer use is how the Bot works a site that has no connector. Plugins are more reliable when they exist. They are also account-wide.

TL;DR

Install from Plugins or an in-chat Connect card. Finish OAuth in your browser. Tokens stay on Cursor’s backend. Team policy can mark a plugin “Disabled by team admin.” Blocking a plugin does not block the website — that takes Network Controls (Enterprise).

Step 1 — Prefer a plugin over clicking

Official stance: plugins beat clicking through a website when a connector exists. Use computer use for the long tail and for tools with no API.

Step 2 — Scope is the whole roster

An installed plugin is available to every Bot you run. Do not connect a send-capable inbox “just for the researcher.” The Reviewer can see that path too.

Step 3 — Sites still fight automation

A site can block automation, expire a session, or demand a human step. The Bot is supposed to hand those steps to you, not work around them.

Stop-line. Do not connect the production CMS, ads wallet, or bank. Read-only analytics and a drafts mailbox are enough for an SMB lab.

Generic drill

15 minutes. Connect one read-mostly plugin you already trust (calendar or a notes app). Ask the Bot to list tomorrow’s events or the last three notes. Confirm it cannot send. If the only plugin you have can send, do not connect it yet — use computer use on a public page instead.
